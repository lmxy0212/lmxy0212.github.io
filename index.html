About

I serve as a Project Manager at Shanghai BTE, where I bridge the gap between complex industrial operations and streamlined project execution.

My approach to leadership is informed by my background at Harvard. As a researcher and creator, I’m deeply invested in how technology can reshape the way people learn, build, and connect. I thrive at the intersection of physical manufacturing and digital innovation, bringing a future-focused lens to everything I build.

I believe that the most efficient systems are the ones that actually empower human creativity (and maybe make life a little easier, too).

Let’s build the future, one project at a time.
