Hi there, I'm Manxueying Li!

As a student in the field of computer science and a self-taught painter, I often found there is a certain similarity between painting and coding. I strongly believe that both hackers and painters are creators who yearn for freedom, and innovation and creativity is always the key to be a computer scientist. As a result, I’m interested in both seeing the world gracefully and logically.

Currently, my research interests focus on the area of machine learning and computer vision, especially fast kernel learning and 3D point cloud semantic segmentation.
